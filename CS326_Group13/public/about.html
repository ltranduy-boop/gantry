What is this?

A cleaner, faster version of SPIRE, the UMass academic portal. SPIRE is slow and hard to navigate, and it tells you nothing about how heavy a course actually is. This site puts that in one place.

What you can do right now

  • Students: add your profile with your name and major.
  • Course Reviews: rate a course's workload from 1 to 5 and leave a comment so other students know what to expect.

Who it's for

Students who don't have easy access to advising or friends who already took the class, especially first-year, transfer, and first-generation students.
